App 4 upgrade brief

Proposed direction for apps/advisor-portal — Editorial Luxury + Editorial Split. Warm, professional paper — not another cyan SaaS sidebar.

Replace

Generic gray admin chrome that fights the brand.

  • Inter → Plus Jakarta + Fraunces display
  • Lucide → Phosphor Light
  • Edge-glued sidebar → floating double-bezel rail
  • 4 equal blue/cyan/purple KPI tiles → editorial KPI stack
  • Bright cyan advisor-* → spruce teal + sage
  • Gray-50 canvas → warm paper + grain

Keep

Advisor workflows that already work.

  • Nav IA: Dashboard · Contacts · Engagement · Training · Team · Presentations
  • Leads / members / commissions / production stats
  • Recent engagement events feed
  • Agency name + advisor role in chrome
  • Auth / password reset flows

Ship order

Highest daily-use surface first.

  • 1. Shell (floating rail + paper atmosphere)
  • 2. Dashboard Editorial Split
  • 3. Contacts + Engagement lists
  • 4. Team / Training / Presentations
  • 5. Login polish
